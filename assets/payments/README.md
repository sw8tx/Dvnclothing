# Payment brand assets

SVG source: [Datatrans payment logos](https://github.com/datatrans/payment-logos), retrieved 2026-11-21. Datatrans is a payment service provider. The source collection is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (see LICENSE); brand names and marks remain owned by their respective owners.

- paypal.svg: assets/apm/paypal.svg
- visa.svg: assets/cards/visa-alt.svg
- mastercard.svg: assets/cards/mastercard-alt.svg
- apple-pay.svg: assets/wallets/apple-pay.svg
- google-pay.svg: assets/wallets/google-pay.svg
- paypal-wordmark.svg: same PayPal paths, presentation card removed and viewBox fitted for the yellow button; no logo paths or colours altered. This adaptation is also CC BY-SA 4.0.

All marks are locally hosted so rendering needs no external tracking/network requests. Google Pay official brand guidance was checked at https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines.

These are display assets, not payment integrations. The store retains its explicit preview/unavailable messaging until merchant onboarding is completed.
